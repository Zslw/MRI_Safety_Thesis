This chapter provides a review of the most important topics to know to fully understand the findings of this current work. It includes an introduction to \gls{mri} scanners, the physics behind them and, more importantly, the possible interactions of the system with matter. Moreover, an emphasis on the static magnetic field of a \gls{mri} scanner and mechanical interactions with epicardial leads is included. A brief discussion on epicardial leads design is included followed by implications for the clinic and the lab setting. 

Most of this chapter content is based on physics and imaging from well known textbooks, including Griffith's Introduction to Electrodynamics \cite{griffiths2013electrodynamics}, and Bushberg's Physics of Medical Imaging \cite{bushberg2011}.
