\subsection{Translational forces observed in a 3T Siemens Magnetom\textsuperscript{\texttrademark} MRI scanner}
\label{sec:resClinicTrans}

% ── CLINICAL TRANSLATIONAL SECTION TEXT ──────────────────────────

In the clinic, four test objects were tested following the ASTM F2052-15\cite{astmF2052} pendulum protocol at the bore entrance of the 3T Siemens Magnetom\textsuperscript{\texttrademark} scanner. A 101 cm pendulum was used, with displacement uncertainty $\delta x = 0.05$ cm. The ASTM F2052-15 acceptance criterion is a force ratio $\mathrm{FR} = \tan(\alpha) < 1.0$ (equivalently, $\alpha < 45^\circ$), which corresponds to the magnetically induced displacement force being less than the gravitational weight of the device \cite{astmF2052}.

Figure~\ref{fig:translational_clinic_res} shows the epicardial lead mounted on the pendulum fixture at the bore entrance. A visible displacement of up to 9 cm was recorded for the Medtronic STREAMLINE\textsuperscript{\texttrademark} 6492 unipolar epicardial lead, making it the most strongly deflected device among those tested.

Figure~\ref{fig:F2052_FR_vs_position} shows the force ratio $\mathrm{FR} = \tan(\alpha)$ as a function of z-axis position along the bore axis for all four devices. The force ratio increases monotonically as the device approaches the bore entrance (smaller z), consistent with the increasing $B|\nabla B|$ product expected from the magnetic field map. Critically, all devices remained well below the $\mathrm{FR} = 1.0$ threshold across the entire measured range. The Medtronic STREAMLINE\textsuperscript{\texttrademark} 6492 unipolar epicardial lead and the 316L stainless steel reference sample reached the highest force ratios, with maximum mean values of $\tan(\alpha) = 0.0931$ ($\alpha = 5.32^\circ$) and $\tan(\alpha) = 0.0905$ ($\alpha = 5.17^\circ$), respectively. The titanium coil and titanium straight rod showed substantially lower deflections, with maximum mean force ratios of $\tan(\alpha) = 0.0092$ ($\alpha = 0.53^\circ$) and $\tan(\alpha) = 0.0040$ ($\alpha = 0.23^\circ$), the Ti straight wire showed minimal deflection making its tendency effectively flat.
